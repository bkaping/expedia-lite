# Selected prompts and decisions — Assignment 3.1

This curated record excludes credentials, personal information, real distress disclosures, and private third-party content.

## Scope and timing

> Design two study-provided skills without changing their canonical steps. Measure the planned exercise from the first exercise instruction to the final planned step, keep it under 90 seconds, and state setup separately.

**Decision:** Pace breathing through six 4-in/6-out cycles (60 active seconds) and present 5–4–3–2–1 grounding as a 45–75-second, no-required-input sequence. Treat preparation and voluntary pauses separately from active practice.

## Support route and control

> Keep an identified route to support information visible across the flow. It must not depend on a model, input classifier, or backend response. Do not force a person to complete or explain an exit.

**Decision:** Put a browser-local Crisis support control in every mockup state and show leave/end actions before and during the exercise. The control is static, independent, and will be localized/checked before implementation.

## Model boundary and stored context

> Give the model a useful but narrow role. The application, not the model, must control canonical skill steps, timing, saved state, exits, and support access. Minimize stored data and avoid a preference-setup burden.

**Decision:** Allow a short neutral acknowledgment only after a completed session and an explicit fictional note. Store limited session metadata plus voluntary notes/acknowledgments; offer the most recent completed skill only as an optional shortcut.
