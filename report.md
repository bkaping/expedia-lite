## Completed behavior and design decisions

The Vue chart displays configurable SMA(5)/SMA(20) and BUY/SELL crossovers. Only completed observed-bar closes advance confirmed indicators; unfinished values are labeled provisional and cannot place orders. FastAPI/SQLite preserve the existing Yahoo live stream, revisions and replay.

Optional forward paper accounts seed indicators without historical trades, then process new closed-candle signals and later received quotes. Persisted event cursors prevent duplicate execution; late corrections do not rewrite prior fills. Starting another account requires stopping the active one. Stops cancel pending orders and retain any position.

Historical backtests use immutable Yahoo hourly snapshots. The engine processes bars sequentially: a signal from an earlier close can fill only at a later available open. Run parameters, dataset identity, orders, fills and equity remain saved and reopenable. Defaults: $10,000 initial cash, long only, fractional units truncated to six decimals, $1 per fill, and five basis points adverse slippage. Decimal accounting includes entry fees in cost basis; realized P/L uses net exit proceeds; open positions retain unrealized P/L without forced final liquidation. [Design, schema and formulas](https://github.com/bkaping/expedia-lite/blob/3cb1c52b26dea7ce42731fda5879eb81290f47fe/docs/design.md).

## Process, limitations and reproduction

One revised approach separated historical next-open fills from forward paper fills: an old interval open could already be known before a live signal, so forward fills now require a later received quote. Tests reject pre-signal receipts and verify the actual quote sets the fill price. A zero-low synthetic fixture was rejected and repaired without weakening dataset validation. [Curated evidence and revisions](https://github.com/bkaping/expedia-lite/blob/3cb1c52b26dea7ce42731fda5879eb81290f47fe/docs/evidence-log.md), [selected prompts](https://github.com/bkaping/expedia-lite/blob/3cb1c52b26dea7ce42731fda5879eb81290f47fe/prompts/selected-prompts.md), and [AI-use disclosure](https://github.com/bkaping/expedia-lite/blob/3cb1c52b26dea7ce42731fda5879eb81290f47fe/AI-USE.md) explain Codex's role. No subagent, new dependency or AI trading model was used.

The strategy avoids look-ahead within its supplied bar sequence, but downloaded history is not a point-in-time archive. A surviving-symbol sample can introduce survivorship bias, and repeated parameter selection risks overfitting. No out-of-sample performance is established. Unadjusted bars, uncredited dividends/splits, gaps, fixed costs and omitted liquidity/latency limit realism. This remains a single-process localhost prototype with no authentication or broker integration. [Startup, automatic SQLite initialization and offline sample instructions](https://github.com/bkaping/expedia-lite/blob/3cb1c52b26dea7ce42731fda5879eb81290f47fe/README.md) require no Yahoo credential; `.env` and generated databases remain ignored.
